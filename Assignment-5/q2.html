Overview

Kyoto is the former imperial capital of Japan, known for its temples, gardens, and traditional wooden houses.

Culture

Home to thousands of shrines and temples, Kyoto preserves centuries of Japanese tradition, from tea ceremonies to geisha districts.

Food

Kaiseki cuisine, matcha sweets, and fresh tofu dishes make Kyoto a destination for food lovers.

Travel Tips

Visit during spring for cherry blossoms or autumn for fall foliage. Public transport makes getting around easy.
